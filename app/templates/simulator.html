How it works: every remaining game gets a win chance from the game model, with players marked out for the games they miss. Each simulated season also draws every team's true strength from a spread of ±{{ "%.1f"|format(result.team_sd) }} points around its projection (wider before the season, narrower as games are played), so long-shot runs happen about as often as they do in reality. Top six make the playoffs; 7–10 go to the play-in. Series are best of seven, higher seed hosting games 1, 2, 5 and 7. Players out for the rest of the season also miss the playoffs. Ties in the standings are broken at random. Lines mark 6th and 10th. Games not on the schedule yet (NBA Cup knockouts, and the games scheduled later for teams knocked out of the Cup) are played against a league-average opponent at a neutral site, so every team plays 82.
